Delete My Account & Data

Last Updated: April 28, 2026 · Required by Google Play Data Safety

PokerDrill is privacy-first. We do not require you to create an account, and most of your data lives only on your device. This page explains how to delete the small amount of data we may hold on our servers.

1. What we hold on our servers

For users who have used the AI Coach feature or made an in-app purchase, we may store:

  • An anonymous device UUID (used solely for AI Coach API rate limiting; auto-rotates every 30 days)
  • Cached AI Coach responses (auto-deleted after 24 hours)
  • Google Play purchase token (used to verify your active subscription/lifetime status)

We do not store your name, email, phone, hand history, or any other personal information on our servers.

2. How to delete on-device data (instant)

You can delete all on-device data right now without contacting us:

  1. Open PokerDrill → Settings (gear icon)
  2. Scroll to "Clear All Data"
  3. Confirm the action

Or simply uninstall the App. Both actions delete your hand history, mistakes, achievements, and all preferences from your device.

3. How to delete server-side data

📧 Email Request (recommended)

Send an email to support@pokerdrill.app with the subject line:

Delete My Account

Please include in the email body:

  • (Required) Your anonymous Device UUID — find it in App Settings → About
  • (Optional) Your Google Play account email if you want subscription cleanup
📧 Open Pre-filled Email

4. What gets deleted and when

DataDeletion Time
Anonymous device UUIDWithin 7 business days of your request
Cached AI Coach responsesAuto-deleted within 24 hours regardless of request
Google Play purchase recordsRetained per Google's policy (typically 7 years for tax/audit). Active subscriptions must be cancelled in Google Play first.

5. What is NOT deleted

  • Records that we are legally required to retain (purchase records for tax/anti-fraud compliance)
  • Aggregated, anonymous statistics that cannot be tied back to you (e.g. total daily API calls)

6. Cancel an active subscription

To stop a recurring subscription, go to: Google Play Store → Account icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → PokerDrill Pro → Cancel subscription

Cancelling here is required before requesting account deletion if you wish to avoid further charges.

7. Confirmation

You will receive a confirmation email within 7 business days once deletion is complete. If you do not receive confirmation, please follow up at support@pokerdrill.app.

8. Questions

For any questions about data deletion, contact support@pokerdrill.app.
